✨ Curiosities

🤝

Ubuntu

A beautiful African philosophy that means: "I am because we are." It is all about kindness, sharing, and community.

💃

Gumboot Dance

A famous South African dance performed with rubber boots! Dancers stomp, slap, and clap to make music with their boots.

🔥

Braai

South Africa's beloved barbecue tradition. Families gather around a fire to cook meat and celebrate together!

🎨 Ndebele Art

Ndebele Art

The Ndebele people of South Africa are famous for their stunning geometric art. They paint bold, colorful patterns on their houses using bright colors and sharp shapes. Each design tells a story about the family and community.
